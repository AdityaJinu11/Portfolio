Tilth AI

AI-driven crop and soil intelligence for farmers.

Many farmers, especially beginners, struggle to choose the right crops because they lack knowledge of soil suitability, crop requirements, and farming practices. That leads to poor yield, wasted resources, and financial losses.

"Tilth" is the physical condition of soil for growing crops. The platform aims to put that intelligence in a farmer's hands: AI-powered soil analysis and accessible farming guidance, in one farmer-first product.

  • Crop disease detection and soil health checks from images.
  • Step-by-step growing guides for 150+ crops, written for people new to farming.
  • Planned around low connectivity: offline-first, regional language in voice and text, and WhatsApp so it stays easy to use.
Stack React.js, MySQL, offline DB, Firebase Firestore
Method Vision models for disease detection. Soil read from images. Agricultural research data and weather APIs.
Who it's for Small and marginal farmers in India, gardeners, and anyone growing plants.
Impact Less crop loss from missed disease, clearer yield decisions from market prices, less spending on pesticides that were not needed.

Eleven beta testers, from students to a practicing agriculture professional, all said they would recommend Tilth. The most requested addition was crop-specific fertilizer guidance alongside deeper disease detection.

Billing System

A Python and MySQL tool for retail checkout, tax, and receipts.

Built as my Class XII computer science project. The aim was a digital billing flow for a small or mid-sized retail or service business: pick products, total the sale, store the record.

The program is a console menu backed by MySQL. Inventory rows hold a name, price, and GST rate. A starter catalog covers a laptop, mouse, keyboard, monitor, printer, and USB cable.

  • Add items to a bill by quantity.
  • Generate an invoice with subtotal, GST, and grand total.
  • Save each invoice as a text file and append the sale to a transaction log.
  • Read past transactions back from that log.
